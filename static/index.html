How to use Silhouette Maker

Hold the mouse over any button or setting for a short explanation.

The steps

  1. Add photos. Side-on, plain bright wall, hair off the face, neck and shoulders in the picture. Each one is cut out automatically and appears in the gallery.
  2. Open a child by clicking their card. Type their name at the top right.
  3. Fix the shape if needed. The orange line is what will be cut. Use Add and Erase to paint parts in or out, Neckline to move the bottom cut, Turn and Straighten if the photo is crooked.
  4. Choose the ornament: its shape and size, and how tall the silhouette is. The picture at the top right shows exactly how it will sit.
  5. Lettering (optional): the name and a second line, straight or curved.
  6. Print a proof sheet from the gallery on plain paper to check everything at real size.
  7. Download the SVG and upload it in Cricut Design Space.
  8. Afterwards, you can delete the photos and keep only the silhouettes, and back everything up.
